Features

Everything a serious IRC client does

Eight reasons IRC Fiber can be your everyday client, on every network you use.

Always connected

A hosted bouncer holds your nick in every channel 24/7, with nothing to install or keep running.

History that waits

Everything said while you were away is there when you come back, and older history pages in as you scroll up.

Bouncer

Bring the client you already use

Every IRC Fiber account is also a bouncer. Settings › Bouncer on ircfiber.com gives you a server, a username and a password, and any IRC client that connects shares the session the web and iPhone apps use.

One login, every network

Your IRC Fiber username reaches every network on your account, at bnc.ircfiber.com:7000 over TLS.

Generate, regenerate, revoke

Create a bouncer password when you need one, and replace or revoke it any time.

History on connect

Clients that support CHATHISTORY fetch what they missed; others get recent lines replayed when they attach.

Locked down

Require TLS, allow only the addresses or ranges you list, and cap how many clients may connect.

See who's attached

Connected clients and recent bouncer activity are listed in Settings, and you can disconnect any of them.

Away when nobody's there

Set an away message for when no client is connected. Your channels stay joined and the backlog is kept.

Why it wins

Nothing to run. Nothing to lose.

The bouncer, the history and your settings live on IRC Fiber, so whichever client you open starts where the last one stopped.

Hosted, not homebrewed

No VPS, no ZNC config, no certificates to renew: sign up and the bouncer is already running.

One session, three ways in

The web app, the iPhone app and your own IRC client attach to the same connection, nick and channels.

Your settings travel

Read markers, pins, highlight words, display settings, per-channel preferences and blocks are saved to your account, not to one device.

Native where it matters

The iPhone app is native Swift at 1.4 MB: it reconnects by itself when the network returns and pages older history from the bouncer.

Trust

No ads. No tracking. Nothing to sell you.

Read the full privacy policy.

No ads or analytics

No advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking, and your data is never sold or rented.

Sign in your way

An IRC Fiber password, Sign in with Apple, Google, GitHub or Codeberg. Providers share only an account ID and an email address.

Report and block

Long-press a message to report it or block its sender; blocks follow your account to every device.

Self-hosted

The bouncer, accounts and history run on IRC Fiber's own servers; the only outside providers are Cloudflare, email delivery and an IP lookup for abuse prevention.

Counts, not content

Activity records hold how many messages you send per day, never what they say, and are deleted after 90 days.

Delete any time

Settings › Delete Account… in the app, or Settings on ircfiber.com, erases your account, networks, history and uploads, and revokes Sign in with Apple.

Get the app

Free on iPhone and the web

One free account for both. The iPhone app is on the App Store; the web app runs in any modern browser.

iPhone

iOS 16 or newer, free on the App Store

Download on the App Store
  1. Open this page on your iPhone and tap Download on the App Store, then Get.
  2. Open IRC Fiber and sign in with your IRC Fiber account, or with Apple, Google or GitHub. New? Tap Create an account.
  3. Accept the Community Guidelines the first time you connect.
  4. Your networks and channels from the web are already there. Tap one to chat.

Updates arrive through the App Store. The app and ircfiber.com share one account, so you can switch between them mid-conversation.

Web

Any modern browser, nothing to install

Create a free account
  1. Open ircfiber.com/register and create an account with your email, or continue with Google, GitHub or Codeberg.
  2. Pick a network from the list, like Libera.Chat or OFTC, or enter your own server.
  3. Join your channels. IRC Fiber stays connected after you close the tab.
  4. On a phone or tablet, add it to your Home Screen to open it like an app.

Prefer your own client? Settings › Bouncer gives you a server, username and password for WeeChat, irssi, HexChat or any IRC client.
